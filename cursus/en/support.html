Support

Last updated September 26, 2026

Need help?

A bug, a question, an idea? The easiest way: in the app, Settings › Help — "Report a bug" or "Contact support" prepare an email with your Cursus and device versions.

Opens your Mail app.

FAQ

How do I sync my iPhone and Mac?
Settings › Sync › Sign in with Apple, on each device, with the same Apple Account.

How do I restore my subscription?
On the subscription screen, tap "Restore". It is linked to your Apple Account.

How do I cancel?
Settings › Subscription › Manage subscription, or in your App Store account settings.

How do I use a promo code?
Open the link you received, or tap "I have a code" on the subscription screen (or Settings › Subscription › Promo code), sign in with Apple, then enter the code.

Notifications don't arrive.
Check they are allowed in your device settings › Notifications › Cursus.

How do I delete my account?
Settings › Sync › Delete my account: all your data is erased from our servers.
