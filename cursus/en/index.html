Cursus

Your homework, exams and reminders, never forgotten

Cursus is a simple, polished app to keep track of what you have to do for school: homework, exams and reminders, with notifications at the right time.

  • iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple Watch, synced with your Apple Account
  • Widgets, Action button and Shortcuts
  • In your calendar (Apple, Google, Outlook…)
  • Custom fields, 10 languages, dark mode

Subscription

Cursus is used with a monthly or yearly subscription, with a 7-day free trial. One subscription works on all your Apple devices.
