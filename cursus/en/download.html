iPhone, iPad, Mac and Android

Download Cursus from your device's store.

App Store (iPhone, iPad)

Mac App Store · soon

Google Play (Android) · soon

You subscribe once, in the app: it works on all your devices signed in with the same account.

On the web: Windows, Linux, Chromebook, any browser

Nothing to download: open Cursus in your browser and sign in with the account you use in the app.

Sign in with Google. Is your account an Apple account? In the app, open Settings › Sync › “Also sign in with Google” (just once): the same account then opens on the web.

Open Cursus on the web

Install it like a real app (Windows, Mac, Linux)

  • Chrome or Edge: once Cursus is open, click the "Install" icon at the right of the address bar (or menu ⋮ › "Install Cursus"). The app then opens in its own window, with its icon in the Start menu or Dock.
  • Safari on Mac: File › Add to Dock.
  • Android or iPhone: browser menu › "Add to Home Screen".

The web version doesn't send notifications while it's closed: use the phone app to be notified.
