Privacy Policy

Last updated September 26, 2026

In short

  • Your homework stays yours: no ads, no tracking, no data selling, no analytics.
  • Without an account, everything stays on your device.
  • With sync (Sign in with Apple), your data is stored privately with Google Firebase in Switzerland (Zurich), and only your account can access it.

Who is responsible

Cursus is developed by Noah Boulos and published on the App Store by Kareem Boulos, Switzerland. For any question about your data: boulos.noah@gmail.com.

Data stored on your device

Homework, exams, reminders, subjects, custom fields and settings are stored in the app on your device. They also power the widgets, the Apple Watch app and notifications, which are all local (scheduled on the device, no server).

Sync (optional)

If you sign in with Apple to sync your iPhone, iPad and Mac, we receive:

  • an account identifier, your email address (or Apple's anonymous relay address if you hide it) and your name if you share it;
  • the content of your homework, exams, reminders, fields and settings, to copy them to your other devices.

This data is hosted by Google Firebase (Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore, europe-west6 region, Zurich). Security rules ensure only your account can read or change it. Google processes this data on our behalf under its privacy terms.

Subscription and promo codes

Payments are handled entirely by Apple: we never see your payment details. The app only checks, on your device, whether a subscription is active. If you use a promo code, we record that your account used it and when, to unlock access.

Calendar

If you turn on "Show in Calendar", Cursus adds what's left to do to the calendar you choose (iCloud, Google, Outlook… depending on the accounts on your device). Cursus never reads or changes your other events, only its own. Nothing is sent to our servers.

AI and third parties

No data is shared with artificial intelligence services or third parties, other than the technical providers above (Apple, Google Firebase).

Retention

Synced data is kept as long as your account exists. A deleted item is marked as deleted so the deletion reaches all your devices. When you delete your account, all your data is erased from our servers.

Your rights

Under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and the EU GDPR, you can request access to, correction, deletion or portability of your data, and object to processing. You can delete your account and all your data directly in the app (Settings › Sync), or write to boulos.noah@gmail.com. You can also contact your data protection authority.

Children

Cursus is made for pupils and students. The app has no ads and no purchases other than the subscription, which goes through Apple (and therefore through "Ask to Buy" if your family uses it). Sync is optional; if the law in your country requires it, ask a parent before turning it on.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above is updated; important changes will be announced in the app.
