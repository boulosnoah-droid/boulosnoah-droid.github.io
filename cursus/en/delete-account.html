Delete my Cursus account

Last updated October 1, 2026

From the app (the easiest way)

  1. Open Settings › Sync.
  2. Tap Delete my account and confirm.

Everything is erased right away: your Cursus account, your synced homework, exams and reminders, your synced settings, the promo codes linked to your account, and the proof of purchase or subscription our server kept for you. Without an account, your data stays on your device: delete it by uninstalling the app.

Without the app at hand

Open Cursus on the web, sign in with the same account, then Settings › Sync › Delete my account. Otherwise, write to us from the app (Settings › Help › Contact support) with the email address of your account: we delete the account and its data.

What is kept

Nothing on our side. Apple and Google keep their own purchase receipts under their own rules; a subscription is cancelled in your App Store or Google Play account.
